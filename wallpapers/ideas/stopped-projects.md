# Stopped projects

What's left of projects that didn't ship. Lessons: [animated-characters](../../docs/approaches/animated-characters.md).

- **Golden Boy** (stopped 2026-11-02): Kintaro cycling through a cedar road. The pedalling mechanics worked, but no version of the rider looked right. A paid image-to-3D test was prepared and never run: [image-to-3d](../../docs/tools/image-to-3d.md).
- **Sonic Dock** (stopped 2026-10-12): Sonic on a pier. 3D rig, puppet warp and image-to-video were all rejected. The project ended because the painted sea already had waves baked in, so added water looked muddy.
- **Cloudsea Drive** (shelved 2026-11-03): an illustrated Porsche above a sea of clouds.
- **Halloween concepts** (2026-10-25): six concepts. The moon cat became [Moon Cat](../published/moon-cat/).
